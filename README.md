LinkWise is a Chrome extension that makes LinkedIn easier to use when searching for people, evaluating profiles, improving your own profile, or exploring jobs.

Describe the type of person or job you're looking for, and LinkWise analyzes the page, gives you a match score, explains why it fits your criteria, and surfaces the most useful evidence without making you read everything manually.

LinkWise can automatically scan profiles and job listings, expand hidden details, combine information from different sections, highlight important information directly on LinkedIn, and update its analysis as more content becomes available.

# What it can do:

## 1. Profile Analyzer

Describe the type of person you're looking for and LinkWise analyzes LinkedIn profiles against your criteria.

- Match percentage and confidence
- Automatically scan profiles
- Analyze profiles as you scroll
- Expand hidden profile details
- Combine evidence across Experience, Education, Projects, Certifications, Honors, Languages, and other sections
- Explain why someone matches your criteria
- Identify strengths and gaps
- Generate a concise profile summary
- Surface descriptive key evidence from across the profile
- Update the analysis when additional profile information is discovered

## 2. Job Analyzer

Describe what you're looking for in a job and LinkWise analyzes the currently open LinkedIn job listing against your criteria.

- Job match percentage and confidence
- Automatically detect and analyze the open job
- Read the full job description and expand hidden content
- Extract responsibilities, requirements, compensation, benefits, work style, location, and other important details
- Explain why the job matches or does not match your preferences
- Generate a concise summary of the opportunity
- Surface key evidence about the actual role and responsibilities
- Organize important requirements and working conditions
- Remove repetitive information so important details are easier to find
- Re-analyze automatically when you open a different job listing

## 3. Smart Highlights

Automatically highlight useful information directly on LinkedIn so long profiles are easier to skim.

- Highlight roles
- Highlight meaningful numbers and measurable impact
- Highlight leadership
- Highlight skills and tools
- Highlight achievements
- Highlight projects
- Highlight languages
- Highlight custom keywords
- Customize colors, transparency, and highlight styles for each category

## 4. Profile Optimizer

Analyze your own LinkedIn profile and get recommendations for improving individual sections.

- Overall profile score
- Top improvement priorities
- Section-by-section profile analysis
- Analyze Profile Photo, Banner, Headline, About, Featured, Experience, Education, and more
- Identify strong, missing, or improvable sections
- AI-assisted suggestions for supported sections
- Generate and improve LinkedIn headlines
- Generate and improve About sections
- Add optional context about projects, certifications, awards, volunteering, leadership, or other experience not currently listed on LinkedIn

## 5. Job Filters

Customize how LinkedIn job listings appear while browsing.

- Hide or highlight applied jobs
- Hide or highlight viewed jobs
- Hide or highlight saved jobs
- Hide or highlight jobs containing custom keywords
- Case-insensitive keyword matching
- Custom highlight colors
- View currently active filters

LinkWise is designed to reduce the amount of information you have to manually search through on LinkedIn and turn long profiles and job listings into faster, evidence-based decisions.
