LinkWise is a Chrome extension that makes LinkedIn easier to use when searching for people, evaluating profiles, improving your own profile, or looking for jobs. Describe the type of person you are looking for, and LinkWise analyzes their profile, gives a match score, explains why they fit your criteria, and highlights useful evidence directly on the page. It can automatically scan profiles, expand hidden details, combine information from different profile sections, and update its analysis as more information is discovered. LinkWise also includes tools for improving your own LinkedIn profile, customizing intelligent highlights, and organizing job listings.

# What it can do:

1. Profile Analyzer

Describe the type of person you're looking for and LinkWise gives a Match % based on how well the profile fits your criteria.

- Auto scan profiles
- Analyze profiles as you scroll
- Enhanced analysis across profile sections
- Auto expand profile details
- Combine evidence from sections such as Experience, Education, Certifications, and Honors
- Match reasons, strengths, gaps, and recommendations
- Key evidence from across the profile
- Update analysis when new profile information is found

2. Smart Highlights

Automatically highlights useful information directly on LinkedIn profiles to make long sections easier to skim.

- Highlight roles
- Highlight meaningful numbers and measurable impact
- Highlight leadership
- Highlight skills and tools
- Highlight achievements
- Highlight projects
- Highlight languages
- Highlight custom keywords
- Customize colors, transparency, and highlight styles for each category

3. Profile Optimizer

Analyze your own LinkedIn profile and get recommendations for improving individual sections.

- Overall profile score
- Top improvement priorities
- Section-by-section profile analysis
- Profile Photo, Banner, Headline, About, Featured, Experience, Education, and more
- Identify strong, missing, or improvable sections
- AI-assisted suggestions for supported sections
- Add optional context about projects, certifications, awards, volunteering, or other experience not currently listed on LinkedIn

4. Job Filtering

Organize LinkedIn job listings while browsing.

- Hide/highlight applied jobs
- Hide/highlight viewed jobs
- Hide/highlight saved jobs
- Hide/highlight jobs by keywords
- Case-insensitive matching
- Custom highlight colors
